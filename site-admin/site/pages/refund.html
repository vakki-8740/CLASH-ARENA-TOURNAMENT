1. Tournament Entry Fees

Tournament entry fees are non-refundable once the tournament has started. If a tournament is cancelled by the platform, all entry fees will be fully refunded within 48 hours.

2. Technical Issues

If you experience technical issues that prevent fair participation, contact our support team within 24 hours. We will review each case individually and may issue a refund at our discretion.

3. Withdrawal Reversals

Once a withdrawal request is processed, it cannot be reversed. Please ensure all details are correct before submitting a withdrawal request.

4. Dispute Resolution

For any disputes regarding refunds, contact us through Telegram or email. We aim to resolve all disputes within 5-7 business days.

5. Chargebacks

Chargebacks or payment disputes without prior communication may result in permanent account suspension.
